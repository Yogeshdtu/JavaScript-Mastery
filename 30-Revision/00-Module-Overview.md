## Is Module Mein Kya Hai?

Ye tumhara **permanent quick-reference** hai. Poora course padhne ke baad tum har chapter dobara nahi padhoge — tum **yahan** aaoge.

Teen tarah ki files hain:
- **Cheat Sheets** — ek topic, ek page. Jab kuch bhool jao.
- **Quick Revision** — poore course ka summary, 4 files mein. Weekly revision ke liye.
- **Gotchas + Final Roadmap** — confusing behaviors aur interview se pehle ka plan.
